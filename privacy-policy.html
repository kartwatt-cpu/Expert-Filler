ResumeFiller Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Summary: ResumeFiller saves the information you enter or import locally in your browser. It reads form-field labels and attributes on webpages only to suggest and fill fields you choose. The developer does not receive your resume data or webpage content, and ResumeFiller does not sell or share that information.

This Privacy Policy explains how the ResumeFiller browser extension (“ResumeFiller,” “the extension,” “we,” or “us”) handles information when you use it. It applies to the extension distributed through supported browser extension stores, including Microsoft Edge Add-ons.

1. Information you save in ResumeFiller

ResumeFiller lets you enter, import, and manage information such as:

  • Name, contact details, address, and other profile fields you choose to provide.
  • Education, work, project, and job-preference information.
  • Answers to common application questions.
  • Resume attachments you upload, such as PDF or Word files.
  • Extension preferences, learned field-matching choices, and settings.

This information is stored locally in your browser using the extension storage provided by the browser. It is not sent to, collected by, or stored on a server operated by the developer.

For store disclosure purposes, ResumeFiller handles the categories of information listed above locally. It does not transmit this information off your device or make it available to the developer.

2. Page access and permissions

To identify form fields on job sites and company career pages, the extension runs a content script on pages using http:// or https://. On those pages, it may read:

  • Form field labels, placeholders, names, IDs, ARIA attributes, autocomplete hints, and field types.
  • Nearby text needed to understand what a field asks for.
  • Whether a field already contains a value.

The extension uses this information locally to suggest matching resume data. It writes a value into a form only when you select a suggestion, use a fill action, or confirm a fill preview. It does not submit applications, bypass CAPTCHAs, or automatically send forms.

The extension requests browser storage access to save your library and settings, and activeTab so the popup can read the current tab’s address for the per-site on/off control. You can disable ResumeFiller for a specific site from the extension popup.

3. How information is used and shared

ResumeFiller uses your saved information only to provide its resume-management and form-filling features. The developer does not collect, receive, sell, rent, or share your resume information, attachments, page content, or field-matching choices.

ResumeFiller does not include advertising networks, analytics services, tracking pixels, or remote configuration services. Resume and document parsing, including PDF and Word parsing, is performed locally by code bundled with the extension.

4. Retention and deletion

Your information remains in your browser’s extension storage until you edit or delete it, clear the extension’s data, or remove the extension. Because the developer does not receive this information, there is no server-side copy for the developer to delete.

You can review and edit your saved information in the extension’s resume library. You can export your data as a JSON file, import a backup, or clear stored data from the extension’s settings. Removing the extension through your browser’s extension settings generally removes its local data, although browser behavior may vary by browser and version.

5. Third parties

ResumeFiller does not require an account with the developer or a third-party service for its core features. It does not transmit your information to third parties. When you choose to submit information to a job site or another website, that site’s own privacy policy and terms apply. ResumeFiller does not control those sites and does not submit forms on your behalf.

6. Security

ResumeFiller keeps data in local browser storage and does not operate a remote database. You are responsible for maintaining access to your device and browser profile. Export files contain your personal information and should be stored securely and deleted when no longer needed.

7. Children

ResumeFiller is intended for job seekers and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the extension or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page shows the latest revision. Continued use of the extension after an update means you should review the revised policy.
